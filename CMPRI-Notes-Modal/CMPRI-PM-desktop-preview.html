Preview Scope, Save Behavior, And Verification

The PM content and responsive CSS come from the authenticated deployed interface inspected on November 3, 2026. The surrounding controls on this page are a preview test harness. No CMPRI services are connected.

Removed: Review And Confirm, Transfer Review, previous-inspection review issues control, and refresh popups. Review directly on each task control. Approving a weekly task finalizes its linked form in the sample draft without opening it. Chief Engineer checks on tasks without a technician submission go directly from blank to green, then back to blank. Technician-submitted tasks cycle blank → red checked → green checked → blank, preserving technician attribution. Red means Needs approval; green means Approved. Managers (including Deon) see the same colors without editing. Technicians see green completion and can undo their own checks. Chief review never changes the recorded technician identity. Draft autosave does not lock checks. Exception: pool-reading completion requires the matching sample daily pool log and cannot be undone after submission; Chief Engineer approval preserves the submitter. Open a notification to focus its exact item and date. Close the modal to save quietly, then reopen to resume. Changed work also saves after five minutes without activity. Further edits start another cycle; activity restarts the timer. There is no polling or database fetching.

Engineer-only examples cover every captured daily, weekly, and monthly control, plus a clearly labeled sample quarterly room control. Repeated daily notifications identify their exact date in the tooltip. These are sample pending checks, not live records. The captured weekly screen, inspection cards, and mobile CSS are preserved. Weekly, Monthly, and History frequency views retain captured rows with sample states. Quarterly Room PMs, the separate Maintenance History workspace, and attached-form layouts were not captured; their panels explicitly identify that limitation. No live data was removed. Existing Google Sheets notices are retained. A mobile browser/device comparison was not possible after the browser debugger connection failed.
