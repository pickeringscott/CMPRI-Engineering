Preview conditions & conditional controls

Approved dynamic bell: 18 × 18 px on desktop and phone, without an outer border. Zero notifications hides the entire red bubble; positive counts show the number. Counts are preview values only. Desktop and mobile: drag a group’s right border to resize horizontally; drag its tab to reorder within its current menu bar. Narrow groups scroll horizontally. Drag buttons left or right within their own tab. For all roles on desktop and mobile, click or tap a tab to collapse or expand its buttons. Hold a tab or button briefly, then drag to reorder. Tabs remain movable in either state. Swipe buttons to scroll on mobile; a short button tap opens its color demo. Button order is saved locally per role and shared between desktop and phone views in this browser, including reopening when local storage is available. This is not cross-device synchronization. Tab sizes and group positions reset when switching role, view, or viewport.
