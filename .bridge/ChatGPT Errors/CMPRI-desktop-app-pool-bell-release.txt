CMPRI DESKTOP APP, POOL STACK AND BELL CORRECTION — NOVEMBER 6, 2026
Baseline: 0e7218b (includes the previous motion and Daily Ops release).
Scope: Scott's desktop-app investigation, Pool Status placement and supplied
notification-bell screenshot. Scott authorized testing and deploying together.

Finding
A pressed mouse moving more than eight pixels before the 320-ms touch-style
hold elapsed canceled pickup permanently. Holding longer after that movement
did not recover it. Consequently neither the dragged copy nor landing/reorder
feedback appeared. The prior gesture tests held the pointer still first and
missed this sequence. A controlled comparison reproduced the failure with the
baseline code in both a real installed Chromium standalone app window and a
separate ordinary browser window. A prototype without the mouse delay worked
in both. This is evidence of a shared gesture bug, not a Windows-only diagnosis.
Scott's Windows Chrome installation was not directly available for testing.
Screenshot review also exposed a second defect: copying computed logical
insets with !important overrode the ghost's left/top coordinates, pinning the
follow-copy to the page corner. Copied logical group margins also shifted its
vertical position after correcting the insets. Earlier checks proved visibility and dimensions
but missed its actual position. The new assertions verify both coordinates
against the trusted pointer events for buttons and groups.
